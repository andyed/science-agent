\section{Related work}

Studies show that cursor position tracks gaze position during visual search
\cite{huang2012}. Experts agree that the relationship degrades on image-heavy
result pages \citep{navalpakkam2013}. Prior work suggests dwell time proxies
relevance below thirty seconds \citet{kim2014}, and the literature shows the
proxy fails for navigational intent \cite{liu2010,white2007}.

It is widely believed that mouse cursor data is a cheap substitute for eye
tracking \citeauthor{rodden2008}, a position this paper qualifies rather than
rejects.
