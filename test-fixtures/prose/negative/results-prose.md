# Results

Per-fold accuracy was 0.821 (SD 0.044) for M4 and 0.820 (SD 0.044) for M3,
demonstrating that removing position features costs nothing measurable. The
retreat-distance term survived ablation, illustrating the mechanism proposed
in Section 3. Two participants were excluded, reflecting the calibration
failure documented in the appendix.

The results suggest that approach geometry carries the signal. The findings
indicate a per-AOI effect rather than a per-page one.
